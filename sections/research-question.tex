\section{Research Question}

In this work, there are several questions of significance. The first two are of most significance, while the problems that follow 
will be explored in what time is left.

\noindent Firstly, we are going to explore the problem of \textsc{FVS} in planar graphs where we try to break all cycles of bounded length $r$. 
Then we move on to the weighted version of the problem and use Baker's technique \cite{Baker} in order to get a PTAS. 
It is important to note that in this work only the cycles of length that is $ \leq r$ are examined and we get a set that contains 
vertices for only those cycles. 

\noindent Secondly, we initiate the study of certified algorithms for \textsc{MWFVS}. More precisely, we provide robust and 
certified algorithms for $(1 + \epsilon)$-stable instances of planar \textsc{MWFVS} with cycles of bounded length, 
for any fixed $\epsilon > 0$. 

\noindent The authors plan to continue this area of research if time allows it, by exploring the following avenues. 

\noindent The first direction is checking if we can translate current PTASs for the \textsc{TSP} to $(1 + \epsilon)$-certified algorithms.
